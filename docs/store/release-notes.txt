Health Glance now runs on the Pebble Round 2.

- Round 2 (Gabbro) is a supported platform, and it gets its own layout rather than a rectangular
  card squeezed into a circle: the card's colour becomes a ring at the glass, the value bar becomes
  an arc of that ring, sleep stages and workout zones become arcs of it too, and the labels centre
  on the axis. Down from Steps still goes home, and Up on Workouts still springs.
- The live heart-rate pulse now follows the shape of the screen: a ring on Round 2, a frame on the
  Time 2.
- Nothing changes on the Pebble Time 2. The rectangular cards are pixel-identical to 1.0.1.

The Android companion is unchanged: there is nothing new to sideload if you are already on 1.0.1.
