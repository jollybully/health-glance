I wanted to see my Fitbit Air data on my Pebble, and I didn't want to use the watch's own health
stuff. So I vibed this out to solve that problem. We truly are living in an age of digital
abundance.

Four cards: steps, sleep, heart rate and your last workout, straight from Health Connect. One big
number on each, the useful detail underneath, and a swipe or a button to move between them.

It's deliberately small. No charts, no paging back through days, nothing to set up on the watch.
I had all of that in an earlier build and never once read it on a wrist.

Got a chest strap, or a watch set to broadcast the standard Bluetooth heart-rate service? The Heart
card shows your live pulse and the screen edge beats with it, at your actual rate. It stops the
moment samples go stale rather than passing off an old number as live.

Needs the Android companion app, sideloaded from the project's releases page, because the watch
can't talk to a strap or to Health Connect on its own. Read-only permissions, no account, no server,
no analytics: it goes phone to watch and stops there.

Health Connect wants Android 8 or newer. The watch app is native C for the Pebble Time 2 (Emery),
22 KB, and the layout is built around that screen, so it's Emery only for now.
